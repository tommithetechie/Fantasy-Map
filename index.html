MAKE IT YOURS

  1. Right-drag (or two-finger drag) to spin the camera, scroll to zoom, and use 3D tilt to drop back to a flat map.
  2. Drag the hourglass along the bottom to roll day into night. [ and ] nudge the hour. P stills it.
  3. Click a golden mark for the cartographer's lore, then Journey here. L opens the quest log. Keys 1–6 fly the chain.
  4. F takes flight: WASD to soar, mouse to look, Shift to go swift. F again to land. The mists pull back as you go. M lifts them.
  5. The small map is the known realm. Click it to journey. Explored ground is remembered on this machine.
  6. Click Export style.json to download this map's style.
  7. Open Maputnik (maputnik.github.io/editor), choose Open → upload the file.
  8. Restyle anything — colors, label fonts, road widths — and export again.
  9. For a true fantasy typeface, self-host glyphs (the Noto fonts here are the only ones the free tile server ships).
  10. POIs come live from the tiles via Overpass API — query real places and re-skin them as inns, quest hubs, dungeons.
