MAKE IT YOURS

  1. Right-drag (or two-finger drag) to spin the camera, scroll to zoom, and use 3D tilt to drop back to a flat map.
  2. The hourglass follows Nashville time. Drag it, or use [ and ], to freeze a clock time. P returns to live time. ?time=22:00 and ?weather=65 do the same from the URL.
  3. Golden diamonds are quests. Open a card, accept, then Fly here or Mark Visited.
  4. F toggles fly mode: WASD glides, Q/E zooms, Shift boosts. Drag, wheel, or touch stops the glide. Pitch stays with the tilt toggle.
  5. The north-up minimap sits at the lower right. Mists are the fog of war. Reset exploration clears them. Effects silences weather, fireflies, and cloud shadows.
  6. Click Export style.json to download this map's style.
  7. Open Maputnik (maputnik.github.io/editor), choose Open → upload the file.
  8. Restyle anything — colors, label fonts, road widths — and export again.
  9. For a true fantasy typeface, self-host glyphs (the Noto fonts here are the only ones the free tile server ships).
  10. POIs come live from the tiles via Overpass API — query real places and re-skin them as inns, quest hubs, dungeons.
